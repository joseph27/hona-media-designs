The man
behind the lens.

HONA Media Network grew out of Ultinity Film — a one-man camera that became a crew. Belal Elhusseiny has spent a decade behind the lens across Europe, North America and Asia, covering delegations, expos and launches where there is no second take.

The method is simple: show up early, shoot like a documentary, cut like a commercial. Every project leaves with a film, a set of social cuts and the behind-the-scenes to prove it happened.

“Being seen is the brief. Being here — in the room, on time, with the shot — is the job.”

— Belal Elhusseiny
