About

HONA Media Network is led by Belal Elhusseiny, a director and cinematographer who has spent the last decade filming for governments, expos and brands across three continents.

Formerly Ultinity Film, the studio was rebuilt around a single idea: presence. Small crews, cinema cameras, and an editor on site — so the story is finished before the room empties.
